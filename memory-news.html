    How to Pack Server RAM for Shipping: Trays, Antistatic Bags and Boxes

    For more than 20 modules, use an antistatic RAM tray. For 20 or fewer, place each module in its own antistatic bag and pack the bags tightly in a sturdy box. Factory packaging is also fine.

    2Rx4 vs 2Rx8 Server Memory: Ranks and Device Width Explained

    In a label such as 2Rx8, 2R is the number of ranks and x8 is the data width of each DRAM device. Neither indicates speed, capacity or quality, and matching rank labels do not make two modules interchangeable.
