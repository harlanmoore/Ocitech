Questions sellers ask

Is there a minimum quantity or lot value?

No minimum — from a few modules to a full data-center decommission. Larger lots can have customized shipment and payment schedules, agreed in writing before the transaction.

Can individuals sell, or only companies?

Both. We buy from businesses, IT resellers, data centers, and individual sellers. You'll get the same process either way.

Do you accept untested modules?

Yes. We test 100% of what arrives, so you can send untested or mixed lots as-is. Untested modules are quoted at a small discount and revised upward if more pass than expected.

How long does testing take after you receive it?

Testing is completed within three business days of receipt for lots up to 500 modules. Larger lots get a written schedule in your offer.

What if I decline a revised offer?

If testing changes a line, you see photos first and can accept the revision or have those modules returned. If you decline a revised offer and request the affected modules back, you pay return shipping. Undisputed lines are paid on the normal schedule.

Do you buy internationally?

We're based in Tacoma, WA and buy memory worldwide. International purchases are handled case by case with seller-arranged inbound shipping to Tacoma — send your inventory first and we'll confirm pricing, shipping, and terms.
