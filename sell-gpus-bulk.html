Why sell GPUs now

Data center GPU demand from AI infrastructure buildouts has pulled prices up across nearly every tier of the GPU market, from enterprise accelerators down to consumer cards that see mining or light compute use. Hardware coming off a lease, a decommissioned cluster, or a wound-down mining operation is worth pricing before it sits unused.

How we price GPU lots

Model, quantity, and condition drive the offer. We look at the whole lot rather than negotiating card-by-card, which means faster quotes and fewer back-and-forth emails for you. Pickup is available for larger lots — full racks or mining farms don't need to be boxed and shipped piece by piece.
