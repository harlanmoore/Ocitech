Questions

About these prices.

How current are these prices?

They are a reviewed snapshot of our current buying offers, not a live feed (last checked: October 2, 2026). Each row shows its own source date. Memory prices move, so we treat these as indicative and confirm a firm number when you send your lot.

Are these the prices you pay me?

These are indicative per-module offers based on recent market bids. Condition (new vs pulled) and volume move the final number — larger quantities may qualify for higher per-module offers.

Why do some capacities say “Pricing upon request”?

Capacities such as 24GB, 96GB, 128GB and 256GB are bought by exact part number and quoted individually, because value varies too much between part numbers to publish a single figure. Send the part number and quantity and we will quote it.

My part number isn't listed. Do you still buy it?

Almost certainly yes. The priced rows are current examples by specific part number, not the complete list of what we buy. Use the search box or send us the part number and quantity for a quote.

How should I pack RAM for shipping?

For more than 20 modules, use an antistatic RAM tray (available on Amazon). For 20 or fewer, put each module in its own antistatic bag and pack the bags tightly in a sturdy box. Factory packaging is also fine. See our RAM packing guide.
