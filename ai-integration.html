Questions

Agentic AI integration — the basics.

What is agentic AI integration?

Agentic AI integration means connecting AI agents into the systems you already run so they can carry out multi-step work autonomously — retrieving data, making decisions within defined limits, and completing tasks — rather than just answering questions. We design, integrate, and operate these agents with human-in-the-loop guardrails and full audit trails.

What AI services does Ocitech provide?

Agentic workflow automation, systems and data integration, custom LLM and AI assistant deployment, and AI strategy and consulting. Engagements range from a single automated workflow to enterprise-wide and public-sector AI platforms.

Can you integrate AI with our existing and legacy systems?

Yes. Most of the value is in connecting AI to what you already have. We integrate with databases, line-of-business applications, internal APIs, and legacy or on-premise infrastructure so agents act on real, current data.

Do you work with enterprise and public-sector organizations?

Yes. Our agentic systems are built for mission-critical, regulated environments where reliability, guardrails, and accountability are essential — including an active AI integration engagement with a national public-sector insurer in Central Africa.

How do you keep AI agents safe and accountable?

We build in human-in-the-loop checkpoints on high-stakes steps, safety guardrails, monitoring, and a full audit trail of every action an agent takes — so deployments stay accurate, safe, and accountable in production.
